## General

### Q: What is CEMK and who runs it?
> college_name: College of Engineering & Management, Kolaghat (CEMK) | department: Institution-wide | category: FAQ-general | document_type: webpage | source_url: https://www.cemkolaghat.in/cemk_aboutus.php | publication_date: not provided | last_updated: 2026-11-01 | academic_year: 2026-27
CEMK (College of Engineering & Management, Kolaghat) is an engineering institute established in September 1998. It is run by the Vidyasagar Society for Integrated Learning (VSIL) under the Department of Power, Government of West Bengal. The Hon'ble Minister-in-Charge of the Department of Power is the Chairman of the institution.

College of Engineering & Management, Kolaghat, P.O. Kolaghat Thermal Power Plant Township, Purba Medinipur, PIN 721171, West Bengal. It is at the junction of NH-41 and NH-6, inside the KTPS campus. The campus spans 32 acres.

### Q: How do I reach the campus?
> college_name: College of Engineering & Management, Kolaghat (CEMK) | department: Institution-wide | category: FAQ-general | document_type: webpage | source_url: https://www.cemkolaghat.in/cemk_howtoreach.php | publication_date: not provided | last_updated: 2026-11-01 | academic_year: 2026-27
By train: get down at Mecheda Railway Station (Howrah-Kharagpur section, South Eastern Railway), then take a bus, taxi or toto; the campus is around 2 km away. By road: reach Kolaghat Thermal Power Plant Township directly, or get down at Haldia More (junction of NH-41 and NH-6) and walk about 5 minutes or take a toto. Kolkata is 59 km away and the trip takes about 1 hr 15 min by road. The nearest airport is Kolkata, 80 km north-east (contact page).
