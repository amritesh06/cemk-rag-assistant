## Extraction limitations
- No local copies: direct downloads from cemkolaghat.in (curl) were blocked by the network proxy, so PDFs and images could not be saved, OCRed or checked for page counts.
- Page and PDF text came through a summarising fetch tool, so prose is often paraphrased and long lists (publications, FDP tables) may be truncated. Numbers and names were returned in full but should be spot-checked.
- robots.txt could not be fetched; politeness was kept by crawling sequentially and avoiding login areas.
- Mandatory Disclosure, General Council and Organization Chart pages rendered no usable text.
- Faculty qualifications are not on list pages; profile pages (about 120) were not crawled.
- Department publication lists (about 160 for CSE, 73 for AIML) and FDP tables are kept in the raw crawl notes, not in the knowledge base.
- Student names, rolls and contacts from training/alumni/committee pages were excluded or redacted for privacy.
- Several sections of the official PDFs (fee images, brochure, NBA letters, bye-laws) remain unread; see CEMK_DATA_AUDIT.md.
