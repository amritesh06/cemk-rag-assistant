## 1. College Overview

### 1.1 Identity, Background and Location
> college_name: College of Engineering & Management, Kolaghat (CEMK) | department: Institution-wide | category: college overview | document_type: webpage | source_url: https://www.cemkolaghat.in/cemk_aboutus.php | publication_date: not provided | last_updated: 2026-10-31 | academic_year: 2026-27
- Name: College of Engineering & Management, Kolaghat (CEMK; also written CEM Kolaghat). Website: https://www.cemkolaghat.in/
- Established: September 1998 (About Us); AICTE EOA also lists establishment year 1998. 28th Foundation Day was held 23 Sep 2025.
- Run by the Vidyasagar Society for Integrated Learning (VSIL) under the Department of Power, Government of West Bengal. The Hon'ble Minister-in-Charge of the Department of Power is the Chairman of the institution.
- Campus: 32 acres inside the integrated township of the Kolaghat Thermal Power Plant (KTPS), managed by WBPDCL.
- Among the select Indian institutions to receive the World Bank-funded TEQIP-II project (Government-Aided category).
- Hosts a C-DAC Regional Centre for the "Finishing School on 3D Printing and Additive Manufacturing Technology", funded by MeitY, Government of India. Home page: a hands-on 3D Printing & Additive Manufacturing course under the MeitY "Finishing School Program", supervised by C-DAC Kolkata.
- Address: P.O. Kolaghat Thermal Power Plant Township, District Purba Medinipur, PIN 721171, West Bengal, India.
- Departments: Computer Science & Engineering (CSE); CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, AIML); Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE); Electrical Engineering (EE); Mechanical Engineering (ME); Basic Science & Humanities (BS).

### 1.7 Location and How to Reach
> college_name: College of Engineering & Management, Kolaghat (CEMK) | department: Institution-wide | category: location | document_type: webpage | source_url: https://www.cemkolaghat.in/cemk_howtoreach.php | publication_date: not provided | last_updated: 2026-10-31 | academic_year: 2026-27
- By train: get down at Mecheda Railway Station (Howrah-Kharagpur section, S.E. Railway), then bus/taxi/toto to the college at the KTPP Township of WBPDCL, around 2 km.
- By road: reach Kolaghat Thermal Power Plant Township directly by bus/car, or get down at Haldia More (junction of NH-41 and NH-6) and walk about 5 minutes or take a toto.
- Kolkata is 59 km away by South Eastern Railway; 1 hr 15 min by road from Kolkata or Medinipur. The campus lies at the junction of the Kolkata-Mumbai and Kolaghat-Haldia National Highways (segments of the Golden Quadrilateral).
- Nearest railway station: Mecheda. Nearest airport: Kolkata, 80 km north-east (Contact page).
- Teachers from IIT Kharagpur, Calcutta University, IIEST Shibpur and Jadavpur University visit for lectures, workshops and seminars (page text).

#### 3.1.3 CSE: Overview, Vision, Mission, PEOs, POs, PSOs

### A. Extraction method and its limits
> college_name: College of Engineering & Management, Kolaghat (CEMK) | department: Institution-wide | category: audit-method | document_type: webpage | source_url: https://www.cemkolaghat.in/ | publication_date: not provided | last_updated: 2026-11-01 | academic_year: 2026-27
- All pages and documents were read with a web-fetch tool that returns a model-processed text of the page, not the raw HTML. Prose (vision, mission, overviews, PDF contents) is often paraphrased; numbers, names and table cells were returned in full but cannot be guaranteed byte-exact. Verify critical figures (fees, intake, dates) against the original PDF or page.
- Direct downloads (curl/wget) from cemkolaghat.in were refused by the session's network proxy (HTTP 403), so no file was saved locally, no OCR was run, and scanned PDFs or images could not be processed. Nothing was done to bypass the restriction.
- robots.txt: the fetch for https://www.cemkolaghat.in/robots.txt was refused by the tool (URL not in an earlier result), so robots rules were not verified. Crawling was sequential and light, and login-protected areas (parent login, employee/member login, grievance portal login, fee portal) were not entered.
- Student personal data: student names appear on the site in training, alumni and committee pages. Student phone numbers and emails from committee tables were removed from this knowledge base, and student name lists from training pages were not copied.
