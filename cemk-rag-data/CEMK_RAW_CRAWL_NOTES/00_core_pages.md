## ABOUT US (https://www.cemkolaghat.in/cemk_aboutus.php)
- Established in September 1998, the College of Engineering & Management, Kolaghat (CEMK), is an engineering institute run by the Vidyasagar Society for Integrated Learning (VSIL) under the Department of Power, Government of West Bengal.
- The college is guided by strong governance under the leadership of the Hon'ble Minister-in-Charge of the Department of Power, Government of West Bengal, who serves as the Chairman of the institution.
- The college is approved by AICTE and is affiliated with Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology, West Bengal (formerly known as WBUT).
- The campus of the college spans 32 acres and is situated within the integrated township of the Kolaghat Thermal Power Plant, managed by WBPDCL.
- The college is among the select few institutions in India to have received the prestigious World Bank-funded TEQIP-II project under the Government-Aided category.
- The college is one of the few institutions in India to have established a C-DAC Regional Centre for the 'Finishing School on 3D Printing and Additive Manufacturing Technology,' funded by MeitY, Government of India.

## HOW TO REACH (https://www.cemkolaghat.in/cemk_howtoreach.php)
- By Train: Get down at Mecheda Railway Station (Howrah-Kharagpur Section of S.E. Railway) and take Bus/Taxi/Toto to reach the College at Kolaghat Thermal Power Plant Township of WBPDCL (around 2 KM).
- By Road: Reach Kolaghat Thermal Power Plant Township directly by Bus/Car or get down at Haldia more (junction of NH-41 and NH-6) and walk about 5 minutes / take a Toto.
- College is in the K.T.P.P. Township, Kolaghat, Dist. Purba Medinipur, West Bengal. Well connected with Kolkata, only 59 km away, by South Eastern Railways. Campus is at the junction of Kolkata-Mumbai and Kolaghat-Haldia National Highways (segments of Golden Quadrilateral). Takes 1 hr 15 min from Kolkata or from Medinipur by road. Scope for interaction with industries around Kolkata, Haldia, Kharagpur; teachers from IIT Kharagpur, Calcutta University, Bengal Engineering and Science University Shibpore and Jadavpur University come for lectures, workshops, seminars.
