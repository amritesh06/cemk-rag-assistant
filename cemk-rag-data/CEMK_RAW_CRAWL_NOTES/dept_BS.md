## Overview, Vision, Mission
- Overview: "The department imparts knowledge of the Basic Science and Humanities" so students can apply them across engineering disciplines; aims to build humanitarian values, environmental concern, management and communication skills, and overall personality development needed by employers and stakeholders.
- Vision: "Pursuing Excellence in Teaching - Learning Process to Produce High Quality Engineering Professionals."
- Mission: (1) To impart knowledge of Basic Sciences for their effective application in engineering and technology. (2) To strengthen analytical, problem-solving, and critical thinking skills among students. (3) To foster communication skills, ethics, and human values for holistic development. (4) To encourage interdisciplinary learning and research for addressing societal and industrial challenges.

## Laboratories (https://www.cemkolaghat.in/dept_labs.php?dept=BS) - 3 labs. Equipment lists and in-charge: not provided for any lab.
### 1. Engineering Chemistry Laboratory
Experiments: (1) Conductometric titration (strength of HCl against standard NaOH); (2) pH-metric titration (strength of HCl against standard NaOH); (3) Viscosity of solutions (sugar solution composition); (4) Chemical kinetics (relative rates of iodide with H2O2, clock reaction); (5) Acid-base titration (commercial caustic soda using HCl); (6) Complexometric titration (water hardness using EDTA); (7) Redox titration (Fe2+ using KMnO4); (8) Heterogeneous equilibrium (partition coefficient of acetic acid between n-butanol and water).
### 2. Engineering Physics Laboratory
- 1st Semester (PH-191): Group 1: thermal conductivity of a good conductor (Searle's method); thermal conductivity of a bad conductor (Lee's and Chorlton's method); dispersive power of a prism. Group 2: Young's modulus by flexure method (bending moment, shear force); modulus of rigidity (static/dynamic); viscosity by Poiseuille's capillary flow. Group 3: dielectric constant; unknown resistance using Carey Foster's bridge; ballistic galvanometer resistance and logarithmic decrement; thermo-electric power of a thermocouple; specific charge (e/m) by J.J. Thomson's method.
- 2nd Semester (PH-291): Group 1: wavelength of light by Newton's rings, Fresnel's bi-prism, laser diffraction; numerical aperture and energy losses in optical fibre. Group 2: Planck's constant using a photocell; Lande g-factor using ESR spectrometer; Stefan's radiation constant; characteristics of photovoltaic solar cells. Group 3: Hall coefficient of semiconductors; band-gap of semiconductors; Rydberg constant from hydrogen/helium spectrum; Franck-Hertz experiment.
### 3. Language Lab (Academic Building W203)
"State-of-art Language Laboratory which is the pride of CEMK." Page states the college was the first in West Bengal, after IIT Kharagpur, to set up such a lab, in 1999. The MAKAUT language lab syllabus was framed by the present HOD. English is taught B.Tech 1st to 4th year using audio-visual and communicative methods.
