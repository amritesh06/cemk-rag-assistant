## Overview, Vision, Mission, PEOs, POs, PSOs
- Overview: B.Tech. course started in 2010; follows MAKAUT curriculum. Mechanical engineering described as an interdisciplinary field covering design, development and construction of physical devices and systems (automatic controls, fluid dynamics, robotics, turbomachinery). NBA accreditation listed for July 2016-June 2019 and July 2019-June 2022 (no later period stated). PG Mechatronics described as integrating mechanical devices, sensors, electronics and controllers. (Paraphrase by extractor.)
- Vision: "Better employability and promoting a culture of research in the field of Mechanical Engineering"
- Mission: (1) Provide quality education that builds students' knowledge, skills and attitude to become technically competent mechanical engineers. (2) Improve employability in Indian and overseas industry, educational and research organisations through continuous evaluation and improvement. (paraphrased)
- PEO-1: Prepare graduates to succeed as mechanical engineers in global professional settings. PEO-2: Apply engineering principles and adapt to new tools for design, analysis and synthesis of engineering systems. PEO-3: Build communication, teamwork, ethical values and commitment to lifelong learning. (paraphrased)
