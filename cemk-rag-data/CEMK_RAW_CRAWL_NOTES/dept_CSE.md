## Overview, Vision, Mission, PEOs, POs, PSOs
- Overview: The department aims to bridge academia and industry and to develop leadership, teamwork, self-confidence and communication skills (condensed).
- Accreditation: NBA accredited (page links accreditation letter https://www.cemkolaghat.in/upload/CSE_NBA_2022_25.pdf and AICTE approval AY2024-25 https://www.cemkolaghat.in/upload/EOA-Report-2024-2025.pdf).
- Vision: Pursue excellence in teaching and learning in key technical domains to produce highly skilled engineering professionals.
- Mission: Produce globally equipped computer professionals and instill moral and social commitment in future generations.
- PEO-1: Build engineering fundamentals for solving hardware and software problems efficiently and securely.
- PEO-2: Provide training in cutting-edge technologies and soft skills for leadership in global teamwork.
- PEO-3: Encourage entrepreneurship grounded in social values and ethics.
- POs: PO1 Engineering Knowledge; PO2 Problem Analysis; PO3 Design/Development of Solutions; PO4 Conduct Investigations of Complex Problems; PO5 Modern Tool Usage; PO6 The Engineer and Society; PO7 Environment and Sustainability; PO8 Ethics; PO9 Individual and Team Work; PO10 Communication; PO11 Project Management and Finance; PO12 Life-Long Learning.
- PSO1: Analyze evolving technologies and apply engineering principles to build computer-based systems.
- PSO2: Adopt changing technologies to solve socially significant problems in a global context.
- Prominent recruiters: Amazon, TCS, Cognizant, DCPL, IBM, Infosys, Innofied, Inolyst, Interra, ITC, Lincoln Electric, LnT Infotech, Macmet, L&T, Mahindra, Rashmi, SCID, United Spirits, Videonetics, Wipro.
