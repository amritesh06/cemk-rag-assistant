## Overview (as condensed by fetch)
- Launched in 2023 with 60 students; intake expanded to 120 in 2024.
- Combines core computer science with AI and ML. Career paths: Software Developer, Machine Learning Engineer, AI/ML Engineer, Data Scientist, Research Scientist.
- Vision: Produce quality software engineers who can adapt to changing IT needs locally, nationally, and internationally.
- Mission: (1) Offer up-to-date graduate programs in IT education. (2) Prepare students for professional careers and higher studies. (3) Promote proficiency in teaching, research, collaboration, and social responsibility.
- PEO1: Successful careers as IT professionals. PEO2: Collaboration in multidisciplinary teams and growth into leadership roles. PEO3: Lifelong learning to keep pace with technology. PEO4: Ethical practice, social responsibility, and sustainable technology solutions.
- PSO-1: Smart programming skills based on analytical, logical, and algorithmic understanding. PSO-2: Assessing social, environmental, ethical, and economic feasibility of IT solutions. PSO-3: Keeping pace with fast-changing technologies such as ML, cloud computing, IoT, and pattern recognition, and managing IT projects.
- Lab/infrastructure highlights: high-end PCs running Linux, one machine per student during lab time; Gbps Ethernet with optical fibre backbone; 24-hour 350 Mbps leased internet; 24-hour lab facilities.
- Key features: industry training; dedicated training and placement cell; co-curricular/extracurricular encouragement; proctors mentoring students.
- Teaching and learning: objective-oriented teaching with regular classes and labs; online attendance monitoring; quizzes, seminars, online tests; remedial classes for weaker students.
- Prominent recruiters: Amazon, TCS, Cognizant, DCPL, IBM, Infosys, Innofied, Inolyst, Interra, ITC, Lincoln Electric, LnT Infotech, Macmet, L&T, Mahindra, Rashmi, SCID, United Spirits, Videonetics, Wipro.
