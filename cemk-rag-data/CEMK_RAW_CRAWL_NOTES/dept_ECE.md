## Overview, Vision, Mission, PEOs, PSOs
- Overview: Established in 2003; focuses on teaching quality and research; faculty and staff aim to develop skilled engineers and researchers in electronics and communication; co-curricular activities (workshops, seminars, expert talks by industry and academia) held regularly.
- Laboratories (overview): Basic Electronics, Analog & Digital Electronics, Microwave & RF Systems, VLSI Design, Digital Signal Processing, Microprocessor and Embedded Systems, and others.
- Software tools: MATLAB, LabVIEW, HFSS, Xilinx, Tanner Tools, and others.
- Vision: To pursue excellence in teaching and learning so as to produce high-quality electronics and communication professionals.
- Mission: M1 Improve graduate employability by fostering creativity and innovation, delivering high-quality technical and professional education, and using continuous performance monitoring. M2 Conduct research through ongoing engagement with research organizations and industry.
- PEO-1: Apply technical and analytical skills to solve complex engineering problems in industry, or pursue higher studies and research. PEO-2: Show professional ethics, communication, and leadership skills in interdisciplinary and diverse teams. PEO-3: Demonstrate innovation and entrepreneurship in developing sustainable, socially relevant technology. PEO-4: Continue lifelong learning through advanced degrees, certifications, or self-study.
- PSO-1: Design and conduct experiments, and analyze and interpret data using modern software or hardware tools, based on core electronics and communication principles. PSO-2: Identify, formulate, and solve problems, applying electronics and communication knowledge to build techno-commercial applications.

## Laboratories (https://www.cemkolaghat.in/dept_labs.php?dept=ECE)
1. DSP, DS & PPS Laboratory - Room W-208 (air-conditioned, LAN); 69.78 sq. m.; 33 students/session, batch 25-30. Courses: Digital Signal Processing (EC593); Data Structures & Algorithms (ES-CS391); Programming for Problem Solving (ES-CS291). Offered to ECE, EE. Experiments - DSP simulation: signal generation, convolution/correlation, Z-transform, DFT/IDFT, FIR/IIR filter design (Butterworth, Hamming, Blackman), overlap-add/save; DSP hardware: assembly on TMS320C6713 kits, VHDL for FPGA DSP, real-time processing; Data structures: arrays, stacks, queues, linked lists, tree traversals, sorting/searching, hash tables; Programming: C basics, matrix/string ops, file handling, dynamic memory, numerical methods. Equipment: FPGA kits (Spartan-6, Virtex-5, Zynq ZedBoard), TMS320C6713 kits, 34 PCs, projector, UPS, printers, LAN switch. Software: MATLAB, Xilinx ISE V14.5, Code Composer Studio, LASI, SPICE, Tanner Tools Pro V6. Lab in-charge: Prof. Debottam Das (DS & PPS); Prof. Dipan Kumar Dey (DSP). Technical in-charge: Mr. Goutam Ghosh.
2. Communication Laboratory (Comm Lab-I) - Room W-202 (AC, LAN); 79.6 sq. m.; 30 students, batch 20-25. Courses: Digital Communication (EC592); Analog Communication (EC491). ECE. Experiments - Digital: PN sequence (7-bit,15-bit), PAM/PCM/DM/ADM, ASK/FSK/BPSK/QPSK, line coding (NRZ, RZ, Manchester), BPSK/BFSK error probability simulation; Analog: AM modulation index, SSB/DSB, FM bandwidth and power, PLL FM demodulation, superheterodyne receiver (selectivity, sensitivity), noise measurement. Equipment: analog/digital trainer kits, communication kits, 100 MHz DSO, spectrum analyzer, function generators, RF amplifiers, CROs, LAN switches, PCs. In-charge: Dr. Seba Maity. Tech in-charge: Mr. Animesh Maiti.
3. Microprocessor & Microcontroller Laboratory - Room W-209 (AC, LAN); 79.6 sq. m.; 32 students, batch 25-30. Courses: Microprocessor & Microcontroller (EC493); Advanced Microprocessor Systems (PC-EE692). ECE, EE. Experiments - 8085: arithmetic/logic, data transfer, searching/sorting, conversion, lookup, strings, LED blinking with 8255 PPI on SDA85 trainer, 4x4 matrix keyboard; 8051: same plus LED timing, switch tasks, Mod-N counter on 7-segment, matrix keyboard, 8-bit DAC/ADC, serial transfer between two 8051s, 16x2 LCD, stepper motor. Equipment: 8085 kits (SDA85H, SDA85M), USBASP programmer, 8051 mounting board, 3-digit 7-segment driver, stepper motor interface, projector, PCs. In-charge: Prof. Debottam Das. Tech in-charge: Mr. Bikash Chandra Bag.
4. Digital Electronics Laboratory (Lab-I) - Room W-201 (AC); 68.85 sq. m.; 30 students. Courses: Analog & Digital Electronics (ESC-391); Digital Electronics (PC-EE492). EE, CSE, IT. Experiments: NAND/NOR universal gates, adders/subtractors, flip-flops, counters, shift registers, 555 timer, multivibrators, Schmitt trigger, amplifier and oscillator design, ALU design, RAM interfacing, BCD-to-7-segment decoder. Equipment: digital IC tester, CRO, DSO, function generator, trainer kits. In-charge: Prof. Soumi Ganguly. Tech in-charge: Mr. Animesh Maiti.
5. Design Laboratory - Room AN-205 (LAN); 62.01 sq. m.; 35 students, batch 24-30. Courses: Digital System Design (EC392); Analog & Digital Electronics (ESC-391); Mini-Project & Design (EC681). ECE, CSE, IT. Experiments - DSD: SOP/POS with gates, flip-flop state tables, decoders/encoders, 4x1 mux, 4-bit parallel adder (7483), sync/async counters, VHDL/Verilog, PSpice MOS/CMOS inverters; Mini-projects: analog (rectifiers, DC supply, BJT/JFET audio amps, power amp, RC/Wien/Hartley/Colpitts oscillators), op-amp circuits, digital logic (BCD decoder, 4-digit frequency counter, MUX/DEMUX, FSM sequence detector, RAM logic), power electronics (single-phase controlled converter, microprocessor-based triggering). Equipment: trainer kits, 100 MHz DSO, function generators, PCs with EDA tools, laser printer. In-charge: Dr. Hirak Kumar Maity. Tech in-charge: Ms. Jhuma Datta.
6. Electronics Devices Laboratory - Room AN-206 (LAN); 59.66 sq. m.; 35 students, batch 25-30. Courses: Basic Electronics Engineering; Electronics Devices (EC391). ECE. Experiments: diode V-I and cut-in voltage, Zener regulation, BJT/FET parameters, CE amplifier frequency response, component familiarization, BJT CB/CE characteristics and h-parameters, self-bias design, JFET drain/transfer characteristics, LED/LDR and phototransistor. Equipment: CROs (30 MHz+), DSOs, trainer kit, DMMs, function generators, PC. In-charge: Prof. Biswajit Dutta. Tech in-charge: Ms. Shipra Bhattacharya.
7. Microwave Laboratory - Room AN-208 (AC, LAN); 125.66 sq. m.; 34 students, batch 24-30. Course: EM Theory & Transmission Line (EC491). ECE. Experiments: antenna radiation pattern, gain, directivity, waveguide impedance, VSWR/reflection coefficient, Smith chart, printed antenna testing. Equipment: Klystron/Gunn diode test benches, vector network analyzer, pyramidal/horn antennas, spectrum analyzers, CROs, DMMs, function generators, PC, projector. Software: ANSYS HF Simulation Suite. In-charge: Prof. Samiran Pramanik. Tech in-charge: Ms. Jhuma Datta.
8. Project & Research Laboratory - Room AN-204 (AC, LAN); 30.6 sq. m.; 32 students. Project Stage I (EC781): literature survey, problem identification, initial analysis, modelling/simulation, interim report, final seminar. Project Stage II (EC782): detailed design and prototyping, system integration, performance testing, thesis submission, research paper preparation. Equipment: soldering stations, breadboards, veroboards, CRO/DSO, function generator, trainer kit, DMMs, PC. In-charge: Prof. Paramita Mukherjee. Tech in-charge: Mr. Bikash Chandra Bag.
9. Analog Electronic Circuits Laboratory - Room N-213 (AC, LAN); 60.3 sq. m.; 35 students, batch 25-30. Course: Analog Electronic Circuits Lab (EC492). ECE. Experiments: clipping/clamping, rectifiers with/without filters, Zener regulation, BJT/JFET/MOSFET characteristics, BJT/Darlington/FET amplifiers, class-B push-pull power amplifier, BJT RC and Wien bridge oscillators. Equipment: CROs, DSOs, trainer kit, DMM, function generators, PC. In-charge: Prof. Dipankar Saha. Tech in-charge: Ms. Shipra Bhattacharya.
