## Overview, Vision, Mission, PEOs, POs, PSOs
- Overview: Curriculum combining theory with practical work. Close association with Kolaghat Thermal Power Station gives students plant visits and live project experience. MAKAUT-based curriculum covering power systems, control systems, electrical machines and electronics, with emphasis on renewable energy and smart grids. Department encourages research, development work and internships. (Paraphrase by extractor.)
- Vision: Produce skilled, value-driven electrical engineers of high quality through excellence in teaching and learning. (paraphrased)
- Mission: M1 Strengthen graduates' competitiveness through a strong academic environment and ongoing monitoring of student performance against stakeholder expectations. M2 Broaden the knowledge base and build a research culture. (paraphrased)
- PEO-1: Graduates pursue productive careers in electrical engineering and related fields. PEO-2: Graduates engage in lifelong learning through further study, professional development and research collaboration. PEO-3: Graduates apply technical competence to identify, analyse and create solutions, with attention to ethics and societal needs. (paraphrased)
- PSO-1: Solve complex problems using professional and analytical approaches. PSO-2: Gain current industry knowledge to become employable in design, research, manufacturing, safety, quality and technical services. PSO-3: Prepare for higher studies in electrical and related engineering fields. (paraphrased)
- POs (titles only): PO1 Engineering knowledge; PO2 Problem analysis; PO3 Design and development of solutions; PO4 Investigation of complex problems; PO5 Modern tool usage; PO6 Engineer and society; PO7 Environment and sustainability; PO8 Ethics; PO9 Individual and teamwork; PO10 Communication; PO11 Project management and finance; PO12 Lifelong learning.
